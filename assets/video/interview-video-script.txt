Career Coach Minute: Talking About Your Portfolio During an Interview
Suggested length: 60–90 seconds

Imagine an interviewer asks, “Tell me about one of the projects in your ePortfolio.”

Instead of saying only that you completed it for class, briefly explain the problem or goal, the approach you took, the tools or technologies you used, and the skills you developed.

Do not be afraid to discuss a challenge. Explain how you responded, what you learned, and what you would improve today. Employers value candidates who can think critically, learn from experience, and communicate clearly.

Your ePortfolio is not simply a collection of assignments. It is evidence of your thinking, your growth, and your readiness to contribute as a professional.

As you continue through your program, update your portfolio regularly. Each project you add becomes another opportunity to tell your professional story.
